Title: Kiosk says INF EXIT
Last modified: 2026-11-12T09:30:00Z
(SYNTHETIC SAMPLE)

Passengers checking in at the self-service kiosk sometimes see "INF EXIT" when
they try to move to an exit-row seat while travelling with a lap infant.

What to do at the counter:
1. Explain that passengers with an infant cannot sit in an exit row.
2. Offer a non-exit seat in the same cabin from the seat map.
3. If the passenger insists the infant was removed, check the booking first;
   the seat map re-runs the check when the infant count changes.
